---
name: Insult rotation
description: Durable anti-repetition rule for the aggressive Discord roast mode.
---

The roast fallback must draw from a broad contextual bank and suppress both exact recent phrases and shared meaningful insult roots. Keeping only a large master list is not enough if the active selector uses a tiny subset.

**Why:** The bot was perceived as repeating roughly 15 insults because the active roast pool was much smaller than the general insult list.

**How to apply:** When changing roast vocabulary, update the active selector and recent-history filtering together; preserve contextual phrasing and keep threats, protected-trait attacks, and real-world harm out of the pool.
